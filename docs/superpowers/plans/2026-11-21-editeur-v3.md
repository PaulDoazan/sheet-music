> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans.

**Goal:** implement `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-editeur-v3-design.md`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-editeur-v3-design.md`

## Global Constraints
- **Écriture** : nothing written is ever auto-modified.
- **Rouge** : red only when "Vérifier le rythme" is on, and only for `under` or `over` measures.
- **Altérations** : `Pitch.alter` stays absolute, so playback and MIDI export need no change.
- **Interface** : French UI. Mobile landscape first. Light and dark themes.
- **Commit trailer** : the same as the previous plan.

## Review Focus
1. A v2 score without `keySignature` or `lineWidths` opens unchanged.
2. Inserting or removing a line keeps every other line's widths attached to the right line.
3. A key change re-keys only the notes that followed the old key.
4. Undo after setKeySignature, setLineWidths or addLine(at) restores everything together.
5. A drag of a barline can't produce a measure narrower than the minimum, or a NaN.

### Task 1: key helpers (`src/music/model/key.ts`)
- `keyAlter(key, step)`
- `VEX_KEY`
- `rekeyPitch(p, from, to)`
- `KEY_OPTIONS` (labels)
- Tests:
  - G major: F → 1.
  - Bb major (key −2): E → −1.
  - rekey from G (1) to D (2): F♯ stays ♯, C becomes ♯, F♮ stays natural.

### Task 2: editor
- `placePoint` and `insertPointAfterSelection` apply the key alteration.
- `setAlter(0|±1)` toggles back to the key alteration.
- `transpose` follows the key.
- `setPitch` applies the key.
- `setKeySignature`
- `addLine(at?)` and `removeLine` splice `lineWidths`.
- `setLineWidths(line, fr)`
- `checkRhythm` ref.
- The snapshot includes `keySignature` and `lineWidths`.
- Tests: one per bullet above.

### Task 3: PageView
- Fixed widths from fractions.
- No filler rests.
- Key signature drawn at the start of each line.
- Accidentals drawn relative to the key.
- `checkRhythm` prop drives the red overlays.
- Offset preview.
- Barline drag with a guide line; emits `resizeLine`.
- ＋ buttons at the top, between lines and at the bottom; emit `addLine(at)`.
- Pure helper `dragFractions(bars, start, end, k, x, min)` in `src/music/render/barDrag.ts`, with tests:
  - clamp to the minimum width;
  - the result sums to 1.
